# Product Overview

## Project Brief — "Overlap" (SpacetimeDB Hackathon)

### What it is

Overlap is an AI-agent networking app for events. Each person has an AI agent
seeded with their profile. At an event, your agent talks to other attendees'
agents, finds common ground, and tells you who you should meet — with a match
score, a short "why you should meet" summary, and suggested icebreakers. You can
take over the agent conversation live at any moment to talk as yourself. Instead
of "add me on LinkedIn," you connect agents.

### The pain point

Networking at events is inefficient and awkward: you meet people at random, burn
time on surface-level small talk, can't tell who's worth meeting, and "let's
connect on LinkedIn" rarely leads anywhere. Overlap does the pre-screening, so
your conversations — in person or online — start deep instead of cold.

### Two modes

- **Offline event:** agents pre-screen before the event; you arrive already
knowing who to find and what to talk about.
- **Online event:** fully virtual; your agent does the legwork and you take over
the chat the moment you feel a spark, connecting fast without meeting in
person.

### How it works (user flow)

Both modes start the same way:

1. Build your agent (short profile: role, what you're working on, interests, what
  you're looking for, what you offer).
2. Join the event.
3. Your agent meets other attendees' agents.
4. Match cards appear on a live shared board — overall score, sub-metrics,
  summary, common ground, icebreakers.

#### Offline event flow

1. Before the event, review your match cards and decide who to find in person.
2. At the venue, walk up to a high-scoring match (use their avatar/summary to
  recognize them) and start a real conversation, primed by the icebreakers.
3. The in-person conversation is the connection made.

#### Online event flow

1. Click a match's avatar on the plaza to open the live agent chat.
2. Take over the chat whenever you want to talk as yourself; when both people are
  live, that's the connection made.

### The plaza (the playful layer) ONLY FOR FULLY ONLINE EVENTS

A top-down, game-like space where attendees appear as avatars moving in real
time. Click an avatar to connect; high-scoring matches show a glowing line
between avatars (the room's match graph made visible). This real-time, playful
world is the wow factor and the main differentiator.
